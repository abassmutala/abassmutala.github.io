### Overview

Pageline Concepts is a branding and creative agency. I designed and built the company’s public-facing website to clearly communicate their services, portfolio, and brand identity while prioritizing performance and maintainability.

### Problem

The client needed a modern, fast-loading website that reflected their brand positioning and could be easily updated with new projects and content without ongoing developer involvement.

### My Role

I worked as the frontend engineer, responsible for the complete implementation of the website from design translation to deployment.

### What I Built

- Fully responsive corporate website

- CMS-driven content structure for easy updates

- Clean navigation and service-focused page layouts

- Performance-optimized static pages

### Technical Stack

**Astro**: Static site generation and performance

**Framer Motion**: Beatiful animations

**Tailwind CSS**: Styling and layout

**Sanity CMS**: Content management

**JavaScript / TypeScript**

### Key Technical Decisions

- Chose **Astro** to minimize JavaScript shipped to the client and improve load times

- Structured CMS schemas to allow non-technical content updates

- Implemented responsive layouts to ensure consistency across devices

### Outcome

- Delivered a fast, production-ready website aligned with the client’s brand

- Improved page load performance and SEO readiness

- Enabled the client to manage content independently through Sanity
